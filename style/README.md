# style

Reference pictures of the real thing: platform signs, departure boards,
station maps, the tape on a platform edge. Not shipped, not read by any
test or build — they are here to be looked at while the board is being
drawn, so the plugin borrows from signage rather than from screens.

The folder is gitignored. Drop images in, name them for what they show
(`sign-green-line.jpg`, `departures-pudong.jpg`, `map-nyc-legend.png`).

The five here are named for what they show: `sign.png` (a platform band,
an Exit sign, the yellow edge tape), `sign2.png` (an Exit sign in three
butted sections), `sign3.png` (a live countdown screen beside a system
map), `entrance.png` (a station entrance) and `plan.png` (the map itself).

What has been taken from them is in `STYLEGUIDE.md`, which is tracked and
names the image behind each rule. The short version:

- A route is a filled bullet with its letter knocked out, and it is the
  primary identifier. Never words where a bullet will do (rule 33g).
- A sign is ONE bar whose sections are butted and told apart by their
  ground, not a stack of boxes with paper between them (rule 2i).
- Sentence case, set bold. Capitals only where the word IS the mark.
- Weight carries emphasis inside a sentence; size does not.
- A countdown row is bullet, destination, quiet sub-line, and the number
  large and ranged right with its unit tiny beneath it.
- Red is for the way out and for what has gone wrong. Yellow is the
  platform edge.
